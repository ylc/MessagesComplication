# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 30, 2026_

Unread Counter for Messages ("the app") is an open-source Wear OS complication that shows how many unread Google Messages notifications are on your watch.

## Data the app accesses

- **Notifications:** with your permission (Notification Access), the app reads notifications from Google Messages on your watch. It uses them only to count unread messages and conversations.
- **System notification tray (optional):** if you grant the `DUMP` permission manually via ADB, the app also reads the Wear OS notification tray. It uses this only to count Google Messages notifications bridged from your phone.

## What the app does with this data

The app stores a single number, the unread count, plus your counting-mode setting, on the watch. It does **not** read message content into storage, and it doesn't send it anywhere.

## Data sharing and collection

- The app has no internet permission and doesn't send any data off your watch.
- It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
- No data is shared with the developer or any third party.

## Deleting data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, removes all data it stored.
